# Junto Contributor License Agreements

This document holds two agreements: the Junto Individual Contributor License Agreement and, after it, the Junto Entity Contributor License Agreement. The form of CLA assistant asks for whom you sign.

- When you sign for yourself, you sign the Junto Individual Contributor License Agreement.
- When you sign on behalf of a legal entity, you sign the Junto Entity Contributor License Agreement for the entity whose full legal name you give in the form, and you confirm that the role that you give in the form lets you sign for that entity.
