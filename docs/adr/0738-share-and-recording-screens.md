# 0738. The screens of a share and of the recording of a call

## Status

Accepted

## Context

ADR 0502 requires one explicit choice of sound for each share. ADR 0601 and ADR 0604 decide that a participant with "record calls" starts and stops a recording, that every person in the call sees an indicator while it runs, and that the start answers at once and the result arrives by event. ADR 0719 keeps a recording in the state of a server only while it runs. The client holds the roles of a workspace and no override of a channel (ADR 0719). This version has no camera.

## Decision

Starting a share:

- The share button of the strip of the call opens a dialog. It shows the sources in three tabs: **Screen** lists the screens with their size, **Window** the windows with their application and size, and **Application** the applications that have a window on a screen, each with the number of its windows on the screen that its share shows, the number of that screen and its size. Under the list is one control for the sound: no sound, all system sound, or one application. With a window, "this application" is the application of that window; with an application, it is that application; with a screen, the person chooses the application from a list. Discord separates the sources of its dialog of a share into tabs in the same way.
- The row of tabs has the role of a tab list, each tab the role of a tab with its selected state, and the list under the row the role of a tab panel with the name of the open tab (`ui::TabRow`). One tab is in the order of the Tab key: the open one, so the Tab key enters the row at the open tab and leaves it to the list. Left and Right move the focus to the tab before and after the focused one, round the row, Home and End to the first and the last tab; Enter, Space and a press open the focused tab. The focus moves without opening a tab, so a person reads the names of the tabs before opening one. The dialog opens on the Screen tab.
- On Linux the dialog has no Application tab: the desktop portal offers a monitor or a window and no source of the windows of one application (ADR 0751). The capture states whether it captures the windows of one application (`ShareSources::application_capture`).
- Nothing is chosen when the dialog opens. "Share" works when a picture of the open tab and a sound are chosen. The chosen picture belongs to the tab that lists it: the open tab of another kind ends that choice, so the picture that "Share" starts is always one that the person sees. The sound "one application" names an application through the picture, so another picture, and another tab that ends the picture, end that choice and the sound is asked again; "no sound" and "all system sound" hold for every picture and every tab. An application that the person picks has the sound of that application when the person has chosen no sound yet: the person who shares the windows of an application expects its sound, and the control shows the choice before "Share", where the person changes it. A sound that the person chose stays. A sound choice belongs to one share and is not a stored switch (ADR 0502). The resolution and the frame rate of the Settings tab (below) apply to a share of every tab.
- Without the permission of the system the dialog states how the person gives it (ADR 0736). On a system without a capture the button is disabled and says so.
- While a share of this device starts or runs, the button has its "on" form, and a press opens the menu "Your shares" under it. The menu lists each own share in the order of its slot ("Your screen", "Your window 2", "Your application", "Your new share" with "Starts…" while it starts) with "Stop", then "Share another screen, window or application" ("Share another screen or window" on Linux), which opens the dialog again for a further share, and "Stop every share". With three shares, "Share another" is disabled and its tooltip states the limit (ADR 0502). The menu closes with a choice, with a press elsewhere and with Escape, and when the last own share has ended. Discord, Slack and Teams offer one share per person; the menu follows the form of their stop control and adds the further share.
- A share that ended by itself, and a share that did not start because three run, are told by a notice in the notice area of the shown view until the person closes it. The close puts the statement away and leaves the shares that run. The notice goes at the press of its close: the calls put the statement away on their own threads, and the window does not wait for them, so the notice is not drawn again in that time. The notice of a later share that ends is drawn again. Test: the window test `call_notices::the_notice_of_an_ended_share_goes_at_the_click_of_the_person`, which gives each state of the calls through the calls of the window, as a session gives it.
- A capture that cannot finish stopping reports a separate cleanup failure in the notice area. The notice states that media sending stopped, that Leave remains available and that the person should restart Junto before sharing again. The detailed cleanup errors stay in the log. This notice can arrive after Stop, including a stop during startup, and does not stop another share. It also remains visible after Leave until the person dismisses it.
- The dialog shows a picture of each listed screen, window and application beside its name, 96 by 54 design pixels with the proportions of the source, so that a person tells two screens of one size apart without trying them. When the system has listed the sources, the dialog asks the capture for one picture per source of every tab (ADR 0736), the screens first, then the windows, then the applications, one after the other, so that a system with many windows is not asked for all of them at once; a system that answers "not supported" is asked once. The picture of an application is the picture of its share at that moment: on macOS a picture with the filter of the share, on Windows a still picture of each of its windows drawn at its place, as the share draws them. A source shows a mark while its picture is taken and an empty frame when the system gives none. The pictures are taken once, when the dialog opens, and are not renewed. They belong to the dialog: when it closes or another layer replaces it, their images are released from the graphics memory of every window (ADR 0774), and a picture that arrives after the dialog closed is not kept. The first version of this record showed no picture, because a preview needs a capture before the person has chosen; a single still picture asks for no more permission than the list of windows does. Test: `calls::the_share_dialog_shows_a_picture_of_each_screen_and_window` opens the dialog with two screens of one size, a window and an application of the synthetic capture, reads the drawn picture of each in its tab (`share-preview-image:display:1`, `display:2`, `window:11`, `application:4001`), and checks that the capture was asked once per source.
- Tests of the tabs: `calls::the_tabs_of_the_share_dialog_are_opened_by_the_keyboard_and_the_pointer` (the Tab key reaches the open tab, the arrow keys, Home and End move the focus round the row without opening a tab, Enter and Space open the focused tab, the Tab key leaves the row, a press opens a tab, and an application picked with no sound chosen has its own sound); `shell::share::tests::a_tab_shows_its_sources_and_another_tab_ends_the_choice_of_the_picture` (also no Application tab without the capture of an application); `shell::share::tests::a_shared_application_has_its_own_sound_unless_the_person_chose_another` (also no sound of an application that started the client, ADR 0706); `ui::controls::tests::the_arrow_keys_move_round_the_row_of_tabs_and_home_and_end_go_to_its_ends`; the window live tests `live::a_person_shares_an_application_and_each_side_names_it_an_application` and `live::sound_help::a_shared_application_has_its_own_sound_and_warns_that_it_can_include_the_call`; `junto-screens` draws `share-dialog`, `share-dialog-window-tab` and `share-dialog-application-with-its-sound`.

The share of an application:

- A share of an application shows every window of one application on one screen: the windows that it has there when the share starts and the windows that it opens there later, at their places, with the rest of the screen left out. The picture has the size of that screen. The screen is the screen that shows the largest part of the windows of the application when the list is read, the first listed of equal ones (`share::listing::application_pictures`, test `share::listing::tests::an_application_is_offered_on_the_screen_that_shows_most_of_its_windows`); an application without a window on a screen is not listed. A window of the application on another screen is not shown, and a window that lies across the edge of the screen shows its part on the screen.
- The frame is the screen, and not the area that holds the windows of the application. ScreenCaptureKit gives the area of the display for a filter of the windows of an application on a display, and Windows uses the same frame, so the two systems send one form of picture. A frame of the area of the windows changes its size when a window moves, opens or closes; each change of size starts the encoder at another size and changes the layout of every viewer. A frame of the screen keeps one size for the whole share, and the windows keep their places in it, as the person sees them.
- macOS: one content filter of the display with the application included and no window excepted (`SCContentFilter` `initWithDisplay:includingApplications:exceptingWindows:`). The system includes a window of the application that opens later, and draws nothing of the other applications. One function of the capture makes every content filter, for a picture and for a sound (`Content::filter`), so the module keeps one call of each constructor of a filter. The capture is built and tested on the Mac (native share tests, ADR 0913).
- Windows: Windows Graphics Capture captures one display or one window. The share starts the existing capture of one window for each window of the application that the list of windows offers and that shows a part of the screen, and the delivery draws the newest picture of each window at its place into one picture of the size of the screen, in the stacking order of the window manager from the back to the front, with the rest black; a fully transparent pixel of a window, as at a rounded corner, shows what lies under it (`delivery::pixels::Frame::compose`, test `the_picture_of_an_application_draws_its_windows_at_their_places_from_the_back`; `windows::rules::application_layers`, test `the_picture_of_an_application_draws_its_windows_on_the_screen_from_the_back`). The picture thread reads the windows of the process of the application again every 250 ms, plus at most the 100 ms wait of its message loop, without reading the program files of other processes, so the reading costs little. It starts the capture of a window that opened or moved onto the screen, ends the capture of a window that closed, was minimised or left the screen, and draws the picture anew when a window moved or changed its stacking order, because a window that only moves sends no new picture of itself. A window that changes its size is drawn at its new size by its capture. The capture of a window that ends, as when the window closes, ends that window only. A window whose capture does not start is left out and is tried again after it has left the list. The module `share::windows::application` has no unsafe code: it uses the capture of one window, the visible frame that the list of windows reads (`DWMWA_EXTENDED_FRAME_BOUNDS`) and the area of the screen that the list of screens reads (`GetMonitorInfoW`), both in physical pixels for the client, whose manifest (of gpui) states per-monitor awareness of the scale of the screens. The picture of a preview is a still picture of each window drawn in the same way.
- A share of an application runs until the person stops it, also while the application has no window on the screen; the picture is then black on Windows. The sound of the share is the sound that the person chose (ADR 0502).
- The track name and the report of the share: ADR 0737.

The stage with a share:

- A line names the sharer and the sound ("with system sound", "with the sound of one application", "without sound"), and at its end what is shared ("Entire screen", "Window" or "Application"), its size and its frame rate. The sharer reads "You are sharing".
- With two or more shares a bar lists them as cards with a small picture, the name ("Carla's screen", "Bruno's window", "Ana's application") and "Watching" or "Watch". A share of an application of another person is named from the state of the server, because its track has the name of a window share (ADR 0737); test `state::view::tests::a_share_of_an_application_is_named_from_the_state_of_the_server`. Two or more shares of one kind of one person are numbered in the order of the bar: "Carla's screen 1", "Carla's screen 2". A share of another person is listed in the order in which it was received, and the own shares follow in the order of their slots. A press on a card watches that share, also another share of the same person. The line then reads "You are watching …" with what the person hears, and "You hear only the share you are watching."
- Tests: the window live test `live::a_person_shares_two_things_in_the_window_and_switches_between_the_shares_of_another` starts a screen from the dialog and a window from "Share another", ends the screen from the menu and then every share, and watches the screen and then the window of another person, with the sound of the watched one only; `junto-screens` draws `share-three-of-one-person`, `share-menu-two-own` and `share-menu-at-the-limit`.
- The line tells when the sound of the own share is closed by a microphone block, and when the viewer turned the sound of the share off.
- The picture is drawn inside the stage with its proportions. Until the first picture has arrived the stage says so.
- Under the picture, a strip shows the people as tiles using the call layout constants. "Hide people without video" applies to it as to the stage without a share (ADR 0735).
- Without a share the stage shows the people as large tiles (ADR 0735).

Settings tab of the call:

- "Your screen share": the resolution (720p, 1080p, source) and the frame rate (15, 30, 60) of the next share.
- "Your view": "Hide people without video", and the volume of the sound of the watched share.
- They are stored in `voice.json` (ADR 0710).

Recording:

- The header of the voice channel of the call shows, after its title: "Record" when no recording runs; the mark "REC" with "starting…" while the server asks the recorder; the mark with the time since the recorder started, "visible to everyone", and "Stop" while it runs; "Stopping…" on that button, described as "The recording is stopping…", after its end was asked for.
- A person whose roles do not hold "record calls" sees "Record" disabled with the statement of the missing permission, and the mark of a recording without "Stop". The server decides each request, because an override of the channel can differ from the roles; a refusal is told by a notice.
- "Record" opens the languages of the speech, and the choice starts the recording with `auto` or with one language. While the recorder starts it, "Cancel" stands after the mark at the place of "Stop" (ADR 0616).
- When a recording of the call ends without a person asking for it, or with a part or all of it not saved, the header shows "Recording failed" or "Recording stopped", "Details" with what happened, the time range that the server has, and what the person can do, and "Record" for a new one. The application reads this from the events of the server, for the call of this device, and keeps it until the person asks for a new recording, a new recording starts, or the call ends. The answer to a request changes only the statement about that request: the event of a failure can arrive before the answer to the request that started the recording. An answer that arrives after its call has ended tells nothing.
- A person outside the call sees the mark without a time.

Structure:

- The application reads every change of the state of a server by itself (the notices of ADR 0719), beside the state, for what the state does not keep.
- "Details" opens and closes with its button, closes with a press elsewhere and with Escape.
- The window derives its view again only after the state of the application told a change. A picture of a share draws the window up to 30 times a second and does not derive.
- An image that the window drew is released when the next picture replaces it.

The notice area is the fixed scrolling area at the end of a call stage, the area above a message list and under its direct-call controls, or the part below the actions of a state panel (ADR 0730).

## Consequences

- A person makes at least three choices for each share: the picture, the sound, and "Share". A further share takes one more press, on the share button, for the menu.
- The share part of the Settings tab holds no sound setting.
- A person without "record calls" in the roles who has it by an override of the channel sees "Record" disabled. This holds until the client reads the overrides of a channel.
- A recording that failed before the device entered the call is not told to it: the events from before the entry are not delivered again.
- The time of a recording is computed from the time of the server and the clock of the machine, as the time of a call is (ADR 0735).
- The recording in the chat of its voice channel and the list of recordings need text messages and follow with them.
- New direct dependency of the desktop client: `image`, in the version that gpui uses, to give gpui the picture of a share.

Every share-ended or capture-cleanup notice carries a unique process-local generation, allocated where the notice is made. The Dismiss button carries the generation of its drawn notice through the window, calls and engine. The window retains the set of acknowledged generations, and each layer clears only a notice with the observed generation. An older failure delivered late is still visible if the person never acknowledged it. A newer failure therefore remains visible even with identical error text and coalesced watch updates. Clearing an acknowledged notice cannot resurrect its same-generation per-call copy. The window tests `call_notices::a_cleanup_failure_is_told_after_an_earlier_share_notice_was_dismissed` and `call_notices::dismissing_a_drawn_cleanup_failure_does_not_hide_a_later_identical_failure` cover active/left calls and a stale drawn dismissal.
